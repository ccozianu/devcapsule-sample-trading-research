#### 1. Why the Gold Debasement Trade Still Has Legs: The Arithmetic of Fiscal Dominance

The traditional macro framework models gold purely as an inverse function of 10-year US TIPS yields (opportunity cost). That relationship broke down after 2022 for two structural reasons:

* **The Sovereign Debt Trap:** With US federal debt exceeding $35+ trillion and net interest payments surpassing defense spending (~$1 trillion+ annualized), the US Treasury is highly sensitive to the average maturity rate of its debt stock. When forced to choose between price stability and Treasury market solvency/liquidity, central banks historically choose **financial repression**—capping yields explicitly (yield curve control) or implicitly (cutting rates into above-target inflation and restarting balance-sheet expansion during liquidity stress).
* **Sovereign Reserve Diversification:** Following the 2022 freeze of Russian FX reserves, non-Western central banks (led by the PBoC and EM reserve managers) recognized that USD and EUR sovereign bonds carry both **fiscal debasement risk** and **geopolitical confiscation risk**.
